## What senior answers show

Strong system design is not a tour of infrastructure products. It is a sequence of explicit decisions tied to product constraints, failure modes, and measurable service objectives.

## A useful structure

- Clarify the user and decision the model supports
- Define quality, latency, cost, and freshness targets
- Establish a baseline before adding complexity
- Design training and serving as one feedback system
- Name the riskiest assumptions and how to test them
