## Start with the contract

Before choosing infrastructure, define what a correct answer must contain, what the system should do when evidence is missing, and which errors are unacceptable.

## Then design the measurements

- Can we measure retrieval recall on realistic queries?
- Can every claim be traced to evidence?
- Do we know which documents were eligible at request time?
- How quickly can we detect a stale or broken index?

## Only then choose the stack

Architecture should follow the failure model. A simpler pipeline with strong observability usually beats a complex one whose quality cannot be explained.
