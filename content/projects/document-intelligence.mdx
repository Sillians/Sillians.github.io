## Architecture

The system combines a durable ingestion layer, layout-aware parsing, vision-language extraction, schema validation, and a human-review workspace. Each extracted field retains provenance back to the source document and evidence region.

## Data Flow

- Documents enter an idempotent ingestion service and receive a durable job identifier.
- Parsing and OCR produce layout-aware document representations for the extraction workers.
- Vision-language models generate schema-constrained fields with evidence references and calibrated confidence signals.
- Validation routes accepted records downstream and sends uncertain or conflicting fields to a review queue.
- Reviewed corrections become audit data and feedback for improving the pipeline.

## Deployment

- Ingestion, parsing, extraction, validation, and review services are independently deployable workers.
- Versioned extraction schemas and model configurations allow a document to be replayed against a known release.
- Dead-letter queues and replay tooling make failed jobs recoverable without manual reconstruction.

## Scaling

- Asynchronous queues decouple document upload from compute-intensive parsing and model inference.
- Workers scale by job backlog and document complexity, while backpressure protects downstream systems.
- Idempotent jobs and checkpointed stages make retries safe when workloads spike or a worker fails.

## Evaluation

- Evaluation measures field-level accuracy, evidence alignment, schema validity, and calibrated confidence rather than document-level success alone.
- Stratified test sets include difficult layouts, low-quality scans, and ambiguous fields to expose failure modes before release.
- Human-review outcomes are sampled to validate calibration and to identify categories where automation should defer earlier.

## Monitoring

- Operational dashboards track queue age, stage throughput, retry rate, review rate, and end-to-end processing time.
- Quality monitoring surfaces confidence distribution shifts, schema-validation failures, and reviewer overrides by document type.
- Audit trails retain source references, model and schema versions, decisions, and reviewer actions for every processed record.
