## System overview

The pipeline combines layout-aware parsing, vision-language models, schema-constrained extraction, and confidence-based review routing.

## Production concerns

- Idempotent ingestion and replayable jobs
- Per-field provenance and evidence links
- Confidence calibration rather than raw model scores
- Backpressure, dead-letter handling, and audit trails

## Design principle

Automation is most valuable when uncertainty is explicit. The system escalates the right work to people instead of hiding model ambiguity behind a single confidence number.
