## The problem

Model quality is only one part of a production AI system. The serving layer must stay responsive under bursty traffic, make cost visible, and give engineers enough telemetry to debug quality regressions.

## System design

- Continuous batching and workload-aware routing
- Model and prompt versioning with safe rollback paths
- Trace-level latency, token, cost, and quality signals
- Load tests that mirror real request distributions

## Engineering judgment

The central tradeoff was not simply speed versus quality. It was deciding where the system could degrade gracefully—and making those choices observable before users noticed.
