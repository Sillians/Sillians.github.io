## Research question

How should a production system allocate extra inference compute when the value of another sample or verification step varies by request?

## What transfers to practice

- Adaptive budgets matter more than a single global setting
- Verifier reliability can dominate generator gains
- Tail latency makes naive search policies expensive
- Evaluation must include cost-quality frontiers, not only accuracy
