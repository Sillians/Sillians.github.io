## Main idea

Useful AI products are rarely a single model call. They are systems of retrievers, models, tools, policies, caches, and evaluators whose interactions determine production quality.

## Questions I keep

- Which component owns each failure mode?
- What can be measured without an expensive human label?
- Where does complexity buy reliability—and where does it only hide uncertainty?
